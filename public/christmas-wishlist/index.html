💌 A Christmas story · Inspired by Tyler's very real wishlist

The Boy Who Dreamed of
the Best Christmas Ever. 🎄

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who believed, with his whole heart, that Christmas was the most magical day of the year.

Every December, he would press his face against the cold window and watch the neighborhood lights twinkle through the dark. He would count the days, listen for sleigh bells that never quite came, and imagine what surprises might be waiting beneath the tree. He didn't dream of being famous or of owning the whole world. He just wanted one Christmas morning so wonderful that he'd remember it forever.

That little boy's name was Tyler.

The years went by, as years have a habit of doing. The boy got taller. The wishes changed. Toy cars became golf clubs; childhood adventures became long evenings cheering for the Packers, building LEGO worlds one tiny brick at a time, and gathering around a table for a game with people he loved. But somehow, the little spark of wonder stayed exactly where it had always been.

And every Christmas Eve, beneath all the grown-up things he was supposed to think about, there was still that same little boy quietly hoping this might be the Christmas. The one with laughter in the kitchen, the familiar sounds of home, a tree glowing against the early-morning dark, and a present someone had picked with him in mind.

“Maybe the greatest Christmas gift isn't the thing inside the box. Maybe it's the feeling that someone knew exactly what would make your eyes light up.” ❤️

Because the truth is, Tyler doesn't need a perfect Christmas. He needs the people who make an ordinary morning feel extraordinary. A thoughtful gift, a shared joke, a game played together, or a little surprise wrapped in paper can become a memory he carries for years.

Still, if you're wondering what might make the little boy inside him gasp with absolute delight… well, a very, very large LEGO box has never hurt anybody.

So welcome to his list. There are small wishes, big wishes, and a few wonderfully unreasonable wishes. Whatever you choose—if you choose anything at all—thank you for being part of his story. That's where the real Christmas magic lives.

And so, dear Santa… 🎅

Here’s to warm homes, full hearts, and the kind of Christmas morning that makes you feel like a kid again. If you're ready to help write the next chapter, the elves have organized Tyler's wishlist by category and price. They have also strongly requested that you not overlook the LEGO section.

Have yourself a very Tyler Christmas. 🎄Made with holiday cheer. Gifts link straight to their retailers. No purchases happen on this site.
